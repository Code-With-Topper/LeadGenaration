Privacy Policy

What we collect

We keep business contact information about companies we may trade with: company name, registered and plant addresses, business phone numbers, business email addresses, website, and statutory identifiers such as CIN and GSTIN. Where a company publishes the name and designation of a business contact, we may record that too.

Where it comes from

From information companies publish themselves — their own websites and public business registries — and from data we are given directly or import from our own records. We do not buy personal data, and we do not collect sensitive personal information.

How we use it

Only to contact businesses about supplying goods and services, and to keep a record of that correspondence.

Your choices

Every email we send carries an unsubscribe link. Using it adds your address to our do-not-contact list permanently, and our system then refuses to send to it. You may also write to us to ask what we hold about your business, to have it corrected, or to have it deleted.

How long we keep it

Until it is no longer useful for business contact, or until you ask us to remove it — whichever comes first.

Contact

Write to us at the address on our correspondence and we will respond.
